Citizens type or speak a grievance in English, Hindi or Tamil. A rule-based NLP engine in the browser sorts it into one of 12 categories, rates its urgency and scale, and points to matching schemes on myscheme.gov.in. An Express and MongoDB backend issues a tracking ID so citizens can follow its status. Built with a teammate.
