A YOLOv8n model fine-tuned on 10 construction-safety classes watches warning and stop zones around a machine, raising an alarm and a stop signal when someone steps too close. It also flags missing hardhats and vests, and runs fully offline. Built for Tata Technologies InnoVent-27.
